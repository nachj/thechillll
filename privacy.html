Privacy Policy — chillll uploader

Last updated: October 5, 2026

"chillll uploader" is a private tool operated by The Chillll (the music artist) for its own use. It connects only to The Chillll's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, with the channel owner's consent.

What the tool accesses

  • The Chillll's own YouTube channel: to upload the artist's own videos and Shorts, set their titles and descriptions, and add them to the artist's own playlists.

What the tool does not do

  • It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person.
  • It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
  • It does not read or modify any other channel's data.

Storage and security

The OAuth credentials for the channel are stored privately on the artist's own server, readable only by the artist's account, and are never shared. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
